# Methodology

Per-category notes on how I approach a fresh box or challenge. What I check first. What I keep in the cheat-sheet. How I keep the thirty-minute timer honest.

These are not playbooks. Playbooks pretend the work is mechanical. The work is not mechanical. These are habits I have settled into after enough boxes that I trust them as a starting point and discard them when the box punishes the habit.
